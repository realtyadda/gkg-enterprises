Common questions about selecting sensors, specifications and enquiries.

How do I select the right sensor?

Start with the parameter, measurement range, process medium, temperature and pressure, installation method and required output. Share these details with GKG Enterprises for a product discussion.

Can you provide product datasheets?

Datasheets should be provided for the specific manufacturer and model once verified. Always check the current manufacturer documentation before ordering.

Can I request a quotation on WhatsApp or email?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 9643394601, or email sales@gkgenterprises.com. Include quantity, delivery location and your technical requirement.

Are all brands and models on the site confirmed?

The site currently explains product categories and selection considerations. Specific OEM relationships, model availability, specifications and certifications must be confirmed before being represented as available.
