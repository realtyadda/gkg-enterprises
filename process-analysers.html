SELECTION GUIDE

Start with the application.

01

Analyse the process

The required analyser depends on the parameter, sample characteristics, required measurement frequency, response time and acceptable operating limits.

02

Online or laboratory measurement

Online analysers can support continuous or scheduled process monitoring. Laboratory instruments may suit sampling-based workflows. The right approach depends on process criticality and maintenance resources.

03

Sample conditioning and maintenance

Some analysers require sample preparation, flow control, reagents, calibration standards, cleaning or scheduled consumables. These requirements should be considered before selection.

04

Selection checklist

Identify the parameter, process medium, measuring range, sample conditions, required response, installation location, utilities, outputs, calibration plan and reporting requirements.

Technical guidance only. Confirm exact specifications, certifications, compatibility, availability and suitability against the selected manufacturer's current documentation before procurement or installation.

Frequently asked
questions.

Does every analyser provide continuous measurement?

No. Measurement mode and cycle time depend on the technology and model.

Should sample conditioning be considered?

Yes. Sample temperature, pressure, particulates and chemical compatibility can affect analyser performance and reliability.

Are consumables included?

This depends on the selected product and supply scope; confirm it in the quotation.
