SELECTION GUIDE

Start with the application.

01

Parameters to consider

pH and ORP; conductivity and TDS; turbidity; dissolved oxygen; and other application-specific parameters. Availability depends on the selected sensor and manufacturer.

02

Where these measurements are used

Water and wastewater treatment, process water, utility systems, environmental monitoring and industrial water management.

03

How to shortlist a sensor

Confirm the parameter and measuring range, sample or immersion arrangement, process temperature and pressure, chemical compatibility, installation method, cleaning requirements, signal output and controller compatibility.

04

What to include in your enquiry

Water type and process, target parameter, expected range, temperature, mounting or sample arrangement, cable length, output/communication and quantity.

Technical guidance only. Confirm exact specifications, certifications, compatibility, availability and suitability against the selected manufacturer's current documentation before procurement or installation.
