SELECTION GUIDE

Start with the application.

01

Start with the target gas

The target gas or gases, expected concentration range, alarm thresholds and potential interfering gases determine the appropriate sensing technology.

02

Environment and installation

Assess indoor/outdoor use, hazardous-area classification, ambient temperature, humidity, ventilation, mounting height and the location of potential leaks. A competent safety professional should define placement.

03

Alarm and integration

Confirm local alarms, relay outputs, analogue outputs or digital communications, power supply, monitoring panel compatibility and event logging.

04

Important selection note

Gas detection is safety-critical. Do not rely on a generic category description to determine compliance, detector placement or protection level. Verify certifications and suitability for the actual site.

Technical guidance only. Confirm exact specifications, certifications, compatibility, availability and suitability against the selected manufacturer's current documentation before procurement or installation.

Frequently asked
questions.

Can one detector identify every gas?

No. Detectors are target-gas and technology specific.

Can it be connected to a central panel?

Only if the selected detector and panel have compatible interfaces and configuration.

What about hazardous-area certification?

Required certification depends on the site classification and applicable regulations. Verify the exact model certificates before procurement.
